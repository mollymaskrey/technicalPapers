## Research Philosophy

I believe useful research starts with a clearly defined question, not a preferred technology.

Whether examining quantum algorithms, AI systems, or computational healthcare problems, my approach emphasizes:

- **Scientific rigor:** Explicit assumptions, testable hypotheses, and reproducible methods.
- **Strong baselines:** New computational methods must be compared against credible established approaches.
- **Practical relevance:** Research should connect theoretical possibilities to meaningful applications.
- **Clear communication:** Complex ideas should remain accessible without sacrificing technical accuracy.
- **Intellectual honesty:** A well-established negative result can be as valuable as a positive one.

Not every paper in this collection represents a completed experimental study. Some are exploratory proposals, conceptual architectures, or research directions intended to stimulate further investigation.
