
# Artificial Intelligence — Research & Applied Systems

**Molly Maskrey | AI Architecture, Machine Learning & Agentic Systems**

This collection presents five selected technical papers exploring artificial intelligence, machine learning, and agentic systems, with particular emphasis on healthcare analytics, pharmaceutical research, and enterprise applications.

My work focuses on combining large language models with conventional machine learning, structured data, retrieval-augmented generation, and deterministic computation to build systems that are useful, explainable, and practical to deploy.

The collection includes implemented applications, experimental prototypes, and proposed research architectures.

## Research Papers & Applied Systems

### 1. AI-Enhanced Medicare Advantage Decision Support: A Multi-Agent Debate System with Local LLMs

**Applied AI | Multi-Agent Reasoning | gpt-oss-20b | Ollama | Healthcare Decision Support**

An implemented prototype for personalized Medicare Advantage plan evaluation using natural-language preference processing, county-level geographic validation, and multi-agent reasoning.

The system translates beneficiary preferences into structured decision criteria and evaluates candidate plans using two complementary approaches: deterministic weighted scoring and an enhanced AI analysis.

The enhanced analysis uses three cooperating agents:

- **Plan Advocate:** Presents the case for a candidate plan.
- **Plan Challenger:** Identifies weaknesses, competing considerations, and potential risks.
- **Arbitrator:** Evaluates the arguments and provides an explained recommendation.

The system runs gpt-oss-20b locally through Ollama, eliminating the need to transmit user information to external language model APIs.

The prototype uses representative plan data and is designed for eventual integration with comprehensive CMS data sources.

**Engineering contribution:** Integration of locally hosted LLMs, multi-agent evaluation, natural-language preference extraction, deterministic scoring, and explainable healthcare decision support.

### 2. An AI-Augmented Patient Explorer Dashboard

**February 2026 | Implemented Application | Local LLMs | Plotly Dash | Synthetic Healthcare Data**

An interactive patient analytics application integrating synthetic electronic health records, longitudinal clinical visualization, and locally generated physician-style clinical summaries.

Built using Python, pandas, Plotly Dash, Ollama, and Meta Llama 3.1 8B, the system operates without cloud infrastructure or external language model APIs.

The application uses synthetic patient records generated by MITRE's Synthea framework. It integrates demographics, diagnoses, medications, clinical encounters, procedures, laboratory results, and other medical observations into a single interactive interface.

A locally hosted LLM synthesizes structured patient information into a clinical narrative, while the underlying records remain accessible for verification.

**Engineering contribution:** A working demonstration of privacy-conscious generative AI grounded in structured healthcare data, emphasizing transparent outputs, local inference, and human oversight.

All demonstration patient records are synthetic and contain no protected health information.

### 3. Beyond Gifts and Influence: AI-Powered Socratic Training for Pharmaceutical Field Representatives

**May 2026 | Applied AI | Retrieval-Augmented Generation | Socratic Learning | Pharmaceutical Education**

An AI-powered training platform designed to improve pharmaceutical field representatives' ability to understand, interpret, and communicate clinical evidence.

Rather than relying on scripted role-play or memorized promotional material, the system uses AI-generated stakeholder personas that engage representatives through Socratic questioning.

Three simulated stakeholders—a cardiologist, endocrinologist, and pharmacy director—challenge clinical claims, request supporting evidence, and encourage deeper reasoning about treatment benefits, risks, and patient selection.

The platform provides two operating modes:

- **Simulation Mode:** Interactive clinical conversations using AI personas and source-grounded retrieval.
- **Oracle Mode:** Direct retrieval of clinical evidence and product information from reference documents.

An automated assessment system evaluates performance across five domains, including patient identification, risk stratification, clinical trial interpretation, benefit-risk communication, and intellectual honesty.

The demonstration uses a completely fictional pharmaceutical product, Cardiovex, with synthetic clinical trials and performance examples.

**Engineering contribution:** Integration of retrieval-augmented generation, Socratic pedagogy, AI personas, and structured performance assessment into an applied healthcare education system.

**[Read Paper (PDF)](field_rep_paper(3).pdf)**

### 4. Hybrid Unsupervised–Supervised Document Coherence Verification Using Restricted Boltzmann Machines and Semantic Entity Graphs

**July 2026 | Research Architecture | Machine Learning | Document Intelligence | Microsoft Azure**

A proposed enterprise document verification architecture designed to identify inconsistencies that conventional proofreading tools cannot reliably detect.

Examples include conflicting dates across presentation slides, discrepancies between chart labels and supporting data, inconsistent client names, and contradictions between claims and evidence.

The architecture combines three complementary approaches:

- **Layout-aware extraction:** LayoutLMv3 identifies structural relationships among headings, tables, figures, and document text.
- **Unsupervised anomaly detection:** Restricted Boltzmann Machines operating on semantic entity coherence graphs identify unusual or inconsistent relationships.
- **Supervised contradiction classification:** Language models evaluate potentially conflicting assertions and supporting evidence.

The proposed deployment uses Azure Synapse, Azure Batch, Azure Machine Learning, and internally hosted open-weight models to support enterprise data governance requirements.

**Research contribution:** A hybrid architecture combining graph-based document representation, probabilistic anomaly detection, and semantic contradiction classification for enterprise quality assurance.

**[Read Paper (PDF)](AI_PROOFING.pdf)**

### 5. Ensemble Methods for Large Language Models: Applying Classical Machine Learning Techniques to Multi-Model AI Systems

**December 2025 | Research Whitepaper | Ensemble Learning | Multi-Model AI | Agentic Systems**

A systematic exploration of how established machine learning ensemble techniques can be adapted to architectures composed of multiple large language models.

The paper investigates four approaches:

- **Bagging-inspired ensembles:** Query multiple models independently and aggregate their responses.
- **Boosting-inspired ensembles:** Use sequential model evaluation and refinement.
- **Stacking:** Combine model outputs through a meta-model.
- **Mixture of Experts:** Route tasks to models according to their specialized capabilities.

The research includes mathematical formulations, Python implementation examples, architectural designs, and proposed experimental methods for evaluating accuracy, robustness, and efficiency.

Potential applications include pharmaceutical research, competitive intelligence, regulatory analysis, and technical document generation.

**Research contribution:** A proposed framework for applying traditional ensemble learning concepts to multi-model AI orchestration, with explicit methods for evaluating model selection, aggregation, and collaborative reasoning.

## Research & Engineering Philosophy

Several principles are common throughout this work.

**AI should augment human judgment, not replace it.** Models can synthesize information, generate explanations, and identify patterns, but their outputs should remain understandable and verifiable.

**Structured data provides the foundation.** Reliable applications combine language models with explicit data models, deterministic logic, and traceable information sources.

**Different models serve different purposes.** Local open-weight models, commercial frontier models, and traditional machine learning methods should be selected according to the requirements of the problem.

**Independent evaluation matters.** Multi-agent debate, ensemble methods, and contradiction detection offer ways to evaluate competing interpretations rather than accepting the first generated response.

**Practical implementation is essential.** Mathematical formulations and architectural proposals establish research direction; functioning applications demonstrate engineering feasibility.
